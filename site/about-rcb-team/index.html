Royal Challengers Bengaluru, from the long wait to consecutive titles

By Sports Desk · 5 minute read

For years, Royal Challengers Bengaluru carried the hopes of a passionate fan base while the IPL trophy remained out of reach. Then the story changed.

A team followed through every season

Royal Challengers Bengaluru entered the Indian Premier League with famous players, memorable performances, and enormous expectations. Every new season brought conversations about leadership, team combinations, form, and whether this would finally be their year.

Supporters continued to fill stadiums and stand behind the team through victories and difficult campaigns. The long wait became part of the identity of the franchise and strengthened the connection between the team and its followers.

The breakthrough in 2025

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won the IPL championship in 2025. The victory ended the long title drought and gave the team and its supporters the moment they had been waiting for.

Champions again in 2026

The story continued in 2026 when Royal Challengers Bengaluru became IPL champions again. Consecutive titles transformed years of waiting into a remarkable period of success.
