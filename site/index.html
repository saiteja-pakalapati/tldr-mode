PERSONAL READING MODE · PROOF OF CONCEPT

Your interests. Your choices. Your reading mode.

This small demo shows how one URL can behave differently according to choices stored in proxy/policy.json. Save a change and reload the article. The proxy reads the file on every request.

EXAMPLE 1

Choose the full article or its author supplied TLDR

Article context. A sports publisher has written a full story about Royal Challengers Bengaluru and has also supplied a concise version of the same story.

"tldr_mode": {
  "enabled": true
}
  • false, the original full article is displayed.
  • true, the same URL displays the concise version supplied by the author.
Open the RCB article

EXAMPLE 2

Skip an article that exceeds your keyword limit

Article context. A productivity article repeatedly uses promotional phrases. The policy says how many occurrences you are willing to accept.

"keywords": {
  "enabled": true,
  "maximum_occurrences": {
    "guaranteed": 2
  }
}
  • false, the original article is displayed without evaluation.
  • true, the article is skipped when a configured phrase exceeds its limit.
Open the productivity article
How to test. Edit one enabled value in proxy/policy.json, save it, and reload the matching article. No proxy restart is needed.
